## dashbord v3.26.0

- Add **End plan** on the Gym Plan page for active or paused plans, with an explicit confirmation before ending.
- Ended plans move to Past plans, retain workout history and templates, and no longer block a new plan with overlapping dates.
- Record the actual local end date.

The app does not end a plan automatically. After updating, use **Gym → Plan → End plan** and confirm it, then review and approve the queued new plan proposal on the same page.
